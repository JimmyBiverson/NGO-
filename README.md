# Community First Uganda

🔗   https://ngosite.duckdns.org/

A modern community organization website developed for **Community First Uganda (CFU)** to showcase its mission, programs, community impact, and opportunities for people to get involved.

The platform provides information about CFU's work in **education, community services, sports and social engagement, and livelihood support**, while also providing features for volunteering, donations, blog content, galleries, FAQs, and contact management.

## Key Features

* Organization profile and mission
* Programs and activities showcase
* Community impact statistics
* Volunteer registration
* Online donation and payment support
* Mobile Money payment instructions
* Donation payment confirmation and receipt
* Blog and community stories
* Image gallery
* FAQ section
* Contact information and inquiry options
* Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

## Project Purpose

The website was built to give Community First Uganda a professional online presence, make its programs easily accessible to the community, and provide digital channels for **volunteers, donors, partners, and beneficiaries** to connect with the organization.
